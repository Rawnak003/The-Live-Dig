How to Request Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your The Live Dig™ account and all associated data in either of the following ways:

Option 1: Delete directly within the app

  1. Open the The Live Dig™ app
  2. Go to Profile, then Settings
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

Option 2: Request deletion by email

Send a request from the email address linked to your account to connect@paramourparadox.com with the subject line “Delete My Account.”

We will verify your identity and process your request within 30 days.

30-Day Deletion Period

After you confirm deletion, your account is scheduled for permanent deletion and will be removed 30 days after your request.

  • Changed your mind? If you log back in to your account within these 30 days, your deletion request is canceled and your account will not be deleted.
  • If you do not log in during these 30 days, your account and associated data will be permanently deleted once the period ends.
  • Once deletion is complete, your account and data cannot be recovered.

What Data Is Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your name, email address, and profile information
  • Journal entries, including text, photographs, and audio recordings
  • Excavation Notes and your responses to Digs
  • Quotes you created or saved, and your favorites
  • Community (Murmuration) posts, comments, and bookmarks
  • Mood and activity data associated with your account
  • Notification settings, usage data, and preferences associated with your account

What Data May Be Retained

We may retain limited information for a longer period where required by law, such as for tax, fraud-prevention, safety, or dispute-resolution purposes. This data is deleted or anonymized once it is no longer legally required to be kept.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active subscription purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Please cancel your subscription through your App Store or Google Play account settings to avoid future charges.
