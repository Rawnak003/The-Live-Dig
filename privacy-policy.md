# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 22, 2026_

We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user.

## Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when creating an account, such as your name, email, and contact details, as well as data related to your activity in the app, including sessions, exercises, and progress tracking. Additionally, we may collect device and usage information, such as app version, device type, and interaction patterns.

## How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to improve your app experience, personalize recommendations, provide session reminders, and deliver coach feedback. We may also use your information to send relevant updates, offers, and notifications, based on your preferences.

## Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or until you request its deletion. 

* **Account Deletion & Grace Period:** When you request to delete your account through the app, your account and associated data enter a 30-day grace period. During this time, your data is retained so that you can recover your account simply by logging back in.
* **Permanent Deletion:** If you do not log in within 30 days of requesting deletion, your account, personal data, and activity history will be permanently deleted from our servers.

## Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell your data to third parties. Your information may only be shared with trusted service providers to help us operate the app and provide our services. We implement industry-standard security measures to ensure your data remains safe and secure.

## Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. You can initiate account deletion directly within the app settings or contact our support team at [connect@paramourparadox.com](mailto:connect@paramourparadox.com) for assistance.

## Cookies & Analytics

We may use cookies and analytics tools to improve your experience and understand app usage. You can manage your tracking preferences within the app at any time.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding your privacy or this policy, please contact our support team at [connect@paramourparadox.com](mailto:connect@paramourparadox.com). Your trust is important to us, and we are committed to keeping your data safe.
